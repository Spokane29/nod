# Agent portals

These are the owner's travel agency accounts. The agency never takes the customer's money: payments and deposits go directly to the cruise line or supplier. Work in the shared Chrome Beta and leave each portal tab open when you finish; other programs read prices from those tabs.

Before you start, read web-browsing with skills. Sign in with sign_in and the saved Vault login for the portal. A portal is signed in only when the page shows the agency or agent name, a sign-out control or booking tools. If the portal asks for a code, a CAPTCHA or new terms, stop and report it; never guess.

Never change a portal setting, profile, password or saved card. Never sign out. Never open a portal's home page just to check a session when a booking or quote page is already open: some portals end the session that way.

## Reading a quote or booking

Open the booking or search the exact sailing (ship, sail date, cabin category, guests and their ages, residency and discounts). Read the full price from the price summary: fare, taxes and fees, port charges, discounts and the deposit amount and due date. Report what the page showed with the final address and the time. Say what you could not see.

## Courtesy holds

A courtesy hold reserves a cabin without payment for a limited time. Place one only when the owner asked for that exact hold, or approved it when you asked.

1. Find the exact sailing, cabin category and guest details you were given. If anything differs from what the page offers (price, category, dates), stop and ask.
2. Go up to the final hold or "create option" control, but do not press it yet. Read the hold terms: price, expiry date and time, deposit amount and due date, and whether it costs anything.
3. If the hold costs money or needs a card, stop: that is a booking, not a courtesy hold. Ask the owner.
4. Ask the owner with one short question that names the ship, sail date, cabin, guests, price, and hold expiry. Press the final control only after a clear yes.
5. After the hold is placed, read the reservation or booking number and the expiry from the confirmation page. Take a screenshot of the confirmation and keep it as the proof.
6. Report: portal, booking number, ship and date, cabin, guests, price, hold expiry (with time zone), deposit due. If the n8n workflow "Save note to shared brain (for Nod)" is available, save the same facts there as a note with project 4vacations, without customer contact details.

Never release, cancel, change or pay for a hold unless the owner asks for that exact action.

## Booking status and deadlines

For each reservation you are asked to check, read the status (held, deposited, paid, cancelled), the hold expiry, the deposit and final-payment due dates and the balance. Flag anything due within 7 days first. Never pay; payment goes from the customer to the cruise line.

## Documents

To get an invoice or confirmation, use the portal's own document control. Confirm the file name, type and booking number before you share it.
